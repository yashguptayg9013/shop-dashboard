Kaveri Imports & Trading

Twelve months of sales, stock and GST for a mobile-accessories and kitchenware trader in Bhiwandi. Every number on this page comes from a SQL view over the shop's purchase bills and sales invoices. Data to 30 Sep 2026.

SQL on GitHub Looker Studio version

Simulated data. The business, its customers, suppliers and GSTINs are made up. A seeded simulation generated the year of bills, modelling festivals, growing and fading products, and an owner who reorders by feel. The SQL runs unchanged on real purchase and sales registers.

Three things the data says

Power banks sell the most and earn the least

They're 17% of sales but 5% of gross profit. The supplier put prices up 8% in February and the shelf price stayed at ₹999, so the margin fell from 10.6% to 6.9% (3.4% online, before courier costs).

Raising the 10,000 mAh to ₹1,099 would take its counter margin from 8% to 16.5%.

See the margin chartThe SQL

₹73,000 is stuck in four items nobody asks for

Micro-USB chargers and cables, AUX cables and wired earphones: 8% of all stock, unsold for 4 to 8 months. ₹1.08 lakh of them was bought this year while sales were falling. The last wired-earphone order (280 units, 13 May) came in when they were selling 60 a month, down from 149 in January.

Clear them to repair shops at cost and take them off the reorder list.

See dead stockThe SQL

Diwali needs ₹5.5 lakh of extra stock, ordered in the next two weeks

On top of everyday reorders, 13 items need festival stock before the rush starts on 18 October. Brass and copper gift sets come from Moradabad, 9 days away, so they must be ordered by 9 October; two are already sold out.

The everyday reorder levels won't catch this. They're set from last year's sales, which is also why the fast-growing 33W charger was out of stock 21 of the last 180 days (about ₹1 lakh of missed sales).

Reorder alerts

Pace is what sold in the last 28 days, divided by the days the item was actually in stock. Order when stock won't last the supplier's lead time plus a week. Quantities cover the lead time plus 30 days, rounded up to the supplier's pack size.

Ran out in the last 180 days

Missed sales = days out of stock × the item's pace on in-stock days. Treat it as the upper end: some customers wait.

Dead: no sale in 90 days. Slow: more than six months of stock at the current pace. Seasonal: a festival item with stock left over, so hold it for the next festival rather than discounting it.

Monthly GST summary

Laid out like GSTR-3B: tax collected on sales, input tax credit from purchase bills, and the cash left to pay. Unused credit carries forward. CGST, SGST and IGST credit are pooled here, so treat it as an estimate for your CA.

How it's built

Stock is never typed in. It's opening stock plus purchases minus sales, so it can't drift from the bills.

1. BillsPurchase bills read by Bill Reader, and sales invoices from the billing app, as CSVs.
2. PostgreSQL10 tables with keys and checks (01_schema.sql), loaded with \copy.
3. SQL views15 views, one per question: CTEs, window functions, a recursive GST ledger.
4. DashboardsThis page, and Looker Studio, both read the views' output.

Data checks

The build stops if any of these fail. A dashboard is only as good as the bills under it.
